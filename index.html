Learn Keyboard with Ease

What it is

Kease trains you to play piano by ear—no sheet music, no note names to memorize, and no theory jargon. The goal is simple: hear a melody or chord in your head and let your fingers find it on the keys naturally, the way you can hum a tune without thinking. Every note is heard in the context of a key established by a cadence, and single notes resolve stepwise back to the home key so your ear feels where every pitch wants to go.

How it works

  • Turn sideways to play: Landscape is the pure keyboard; portrait holds your guide and settings. Lying down? Hold the phone upside down (cameras at the bottom) and iPhone keeps the full-size landscape keyboard open on either side.
  • Cadence & resolution: A short chord cadence sets the home key. On single-note lessons, each note resolves stepwise back to the nearest home key—lighting up the piano keys as it walks home.
  • Hunt → Clean on every step: Every new skill opens in Hunt so you can tap around freely to find the sound by ear. Once it clicks, it automatically tightens to Clean (one-shot) for two-day 90% mastery.
  • Jam & Free Play: Tap Jam for a warm backing groove over the active keys, or Free Play to explore the full keyboard with octave arrows and note names.
