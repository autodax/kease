How it works

  • Seamless progress: In Auto · Adaptive, new keys, rhythms, and longer phrases join smoothly as your ear gets surer—with easier review sprinkled in for momentum.
  • Hunt by ear on new skills: Whenever a new key or phrase length arrives, tap around freely to find the sound by ear. Once it clicks, it gently tightens to one-shot guesses.
  • Jam & Free Play: Tap Jam for a warm backing groove over the active keys, or Free Play to open all two octaves with subtle note names.
  • Playing lying down: Tap the lock icon in landscape to keep the keyboard open if your phone tilts.
